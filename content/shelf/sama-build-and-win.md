> You can skip all the parties, all the conferences, all the press, all the tweets. Build a great product and get users and win.

Harshil shared this on LinkedIn just before GFF, under his own reminder for founders and builders during GFF week: go to the talks, meet people, exchange ideas, do the fancy demos, have fun — just don't confuse any of it with the actual work.

Quite inspiring, especially because as a CEO he would usually have encouraged events like these pre-AI. With AI he is himself building and shipping regularly, and I loved his stance that shipping is all that matters.
