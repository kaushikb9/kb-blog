I am usually in this spiral loop myself. I have been many years in the industry and by now it's assumed that I have figured these things out. Leaders usually say these things to their teams — I did too — but more than that, I was thinking I also need to stop spiralling and just do what I do best: put my head down and do the work. Yes, I need to keep showing up and create visibility from time to time, but delivering continuously matters more than anything.
